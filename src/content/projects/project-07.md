Made for the Spokane Global Game Jam in a team of 5. We built a fully-functional game in just a single weekend. <br>
It turned out to be quite a successful gamejam. I had the pleasure of working with a wonderful team. <br>
You play as a hero on a <i>"The Bachelor"</i> style game show for dating other superheroes. <br>
Players play as a superhero going through a series of minigames as they attempt to overcome the mind control of the nefarious villains trying to make them fail the game show. <br>
At the end they are rewarded with a sultry, but safe for work, scene of two loving superheroes.
<br>
You can play it online [here](https://stevenharperja.itch.io/make-all-super-heroes-kiss) using the password "MASK". I recommend playing it fullscreen.
