A collection of shaders to create foil effects for cards in Tabletop Simulator or other Unity projects.<br><br>

This was a collection of all of the shaders I had been experimenting with. 

I noticed that no one had published a comprehensive guide or tool for how to make foil cards in tabletop simulator, so I made one that would be easy for the average player to use.
